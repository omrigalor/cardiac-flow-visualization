# Visual review and limitations
Current review scope: anatomy silhouette/framing, open/closing/closed leaflet states, presence and direction of illustrative cell flow, before/after visibility, scene timing, and output integrity. These are art/engineering checks, not medical validation.

Source geometry is a reference atlas, not a textured specimen scan. Current procedural shading and supplemental vessels do not meet the user's requested “indistinguishable from a real human heart” standard. The anatomical cutaway and generic frame remain stylized. The supplied historical movie was inspected as a benchmark; its footage and proprietary device mechanism were not copied.

A fixed anterior camera preserves orientation during the medical explanation. A dedicated slow 360-degree anatomical-X orbit with modest Y motion is confined to the final hero sequence. Most of the ventricular silhouette remains visible. A faint anterior right-heart wall provides context while exposing the implant. Three deforming leaflets and exaggerated biconcave blood cells illustrate diastole and systole.

Known review needs before external use: cardiologist/medical-illustrator review of annular registration and supplemental vessels; verified proprietary device CAD if a product-specific representation is required; patient/specimen-quality tissue surface work for higher realism; review the partial transparent wall and cell legibility at presentation size. The frame is intentionally exposed by the cutaway, not a depiction of missing native support.

No quantitative clinical-performance claims, pressure values, or validated computational fields are present. No assertion is made that this version is visually superior to the historical reference.
